SIGHTLINE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

SightLine is a companion app for Marvel Rivals, distributed through the Overwolf platform. It is made by Abdulaziz Al khaldi (Gamers Academy), Saudi Arabia ("we"). This policy explains what data SightLine uses and why.

What SightLine reads

  • Live game data from Overwolf. While Marvel Rivals runs, Overwolf's Game Events Provider gives SightLine the match lobby: player names and IDs, heroes, team, map, mode, bans and the match result. SightLine uses this only to show the lobby panel and your post-match view. SightLine does not read game memory or files.
  • Public player statistics. SightLine looks up public profiles (rank, matches, heroes) for you and the players in your lobby on RivalsMeta, a public Marvel Rivals statistics site. Only the in-game player ID is sent in these lookups. Players who hide their profile in game show as "Private".
  • Your settings. Your player ID and preferences are saved in a file on your PC.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers that collect your data, and we have no user accounts.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not record your screen, microphone, chat or keystrokes.

Advertising and subscriptions

The free version shows ads served by Overwolf. Overwolf and its ad partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show and measure ads. On first run you get a consent screen (CMP) where you can accept or refuse personalised ads, and you can change this later in Settings. Overwolf's handling of this data is covered by the Overwolf Privacy Policy.

Paid subscriptions are sold through Overwolf Subscriptions with Tebex as the payment provider. We never see your card details. Tebex's own privacy policy applies to payments.

Cached data

To keep lookups fast and light, SightLine keeps copies of public statistics in a cache folder on your PC for a short time (about 2 minutes for profiles, 6 hours for hero stats). Uninstalling SightLine removes the app. You can delete the cache any time from %APPDATA%\SightLine.

Children

SightLine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Because we do not store your data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete beyond what is on your PC. For ad data, use the consent screen in Settings or contact Overwolf. For anything else, email us.
